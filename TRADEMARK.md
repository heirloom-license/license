# Trademark and marks policy

The Heirloom License is designed to be copied — its text is dedicated to the
public domain (CC0 1.0). But for the license to *mean* something to a buyer, the
name and badge have to reliably point to one specific, unmodified set of terms.
So the text is free and the marks are reserved. This is the same split used by
the Business Source License and the Functional Source License.

## The marks

- The name **"Heirloom License"** and the **"Heirloom"** name as applied to this
  license.
- The **Heirloom badge** (the artwork in `badges/`).

These are marks of the Heirloom License project. They are **not** licensed under
CC0.

## Permitted uses (no permission needed)

- Stating that your software "is released under the Heirloom License" — when it
  genuinely is, under an **unmodified, published version**.
- Displaying the badge on that software's site, README, and About box.
- Linking to https://heirloomlicense.org.
- Referring to the license by name in articles, talks, and documentation.

## Prohibited uses

- Using the name "Heirloom License" or the badge for software that is **not**
  under an unmodified version of the license.
- Applying the name or badge to a **modified** version of the license text. You
  may fork and modify the text (it's CC0), but a modified license must carry a
  **different name** and must not use the Heirloom name or badge in any way likely
  to cause confusion about the terms.
- Using the marks in a way that suggests endorsement or official status without
  permission.

## Why forking the text is fine but renaming is required

Anyone may copy the words. What they may not do is call different terms by the
same name — because the value to buyers is that "Heirloom License" always means
the same promise. Rename your variant and the commons stays trustworthy.

## Registration

The marks are currently **unregistered** (common-law). Formal registration
(e.g. USPTO) is under consideration and tracked in the project's legal-review
issues. This policy applies regardless of registration status.
